# Money Heist (La Casa de Papel)

## About the Series

**Money Heist** is a Spanish *heist crime series* created by **Álex Pina**. It first aired in Spain in 2017 and became a global hit after **Netflix** re-released it. The story follows a group of robbers who plan and carry out huge heists with a mastermind known as **The Professor**.
